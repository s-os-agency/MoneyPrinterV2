# Execution partner negotiation options

Status: proposal for negotiation, not an accepted contract. Version 2026-11-15.

The owner provides the project concept, work completed to date, and agreed
infrastructure. The designated management agent handles client coordination;
the owner does not undertake day-to-day client relationship management.

The recipient may choose either option, propose another arrangement, or decline.
Reviewing documentation, claiming a task, or saying "willing to work" does not
by itself accept a financial guarantee. Identify the responsible contracting
entity and an authorized representative for any binding response.

## Shared commercial proposal

- Execution contributors share one project pool: proposed tiers of 20%, 25%,
  and a maximum of 30%, not 30% per contributor.
- A separate 5% management share is additional to that pool and is not deducted
  from the contributors' share. At the maximum the combined shares are 35%.
- Both percentages use collected project revenue after actual platform fees.
  Define any further deduction explicitly; do not add it silently.
- Contributors propose cumulative realized-profit thresholds for the tiers.
  Measure that profit before contributor/management distributions to avoid a
  circular calculation. Specify the measurement window and when a tier applies.
- The existing API authorization is a maximum of USD50/month. This is a spending
  ceiling, not a promised top-up or an obligation for the owner to pay every month.
- No open-ended obligation for the owner to fund operating deficits. State who
  funds each service and infrastructure item and how collected cash funds work.
- Collaboration across platforms is welcome. Record all contributors against a
  project ID; record each customer payment once. Separate independent customer
  orders for the same service are full, separate sales.
- The published success model is a 100%-success what-if illustration. The
  recipient prepares its own numbers; no modeled amount is represented as a
  signed customer contract, guaranteed demand, or received income.

## Option A — financial-result commitment with expense reimbursement

The recipient reviews the documentation, prepares its own operating calculation,
and proposes a specific financial result within 30 days of the agreed start.
It expressly accepts the financial-result commitment as well as execution.

Proposed acceptance text:

> We reviewed the project documentation and prepared our own calculations. We
> commit to the financial result and measurement method in our completed offer
> within 30 days of the agreed start date. If that result is not achieved, we
> commit, at minimum, to reimburse the owner's documented service and
> infrastructure expenses included in the agreed project expense schedule.
> The completed schedule identifies the covered services, measurement period,
> amounts or calculation basis, and reimbursement due date. Any additional
> compensation is stated separately in our accepted offer.

The minimum reimbursement here is the included, documented project expense
schedule; it is not an arbitrary cap or a promise of unmeasured lost profits.
The result may be defined as a collected-revenue amount or a net financial
result; specify which and its deductions before accepting.

## Option B — execution commitment without a market-result guarantee

The recipient proposes specific outputs, delivery dates, staffing and operation
volumes, with compensation from collected project revenue under the shared terms.
It does not guarantee customer demand or the financial result.

This option must still address the owner's funding concern: propose funding
from cleared project receipts, supplier-funded operating costs, or another
explicitly agreed arrangement. It must not assume indefinite personal top-ups.
State how any owner-approved prepaid cost for an undelivered output is returned
or remedied. Do not present this as the financial-result guarantee in Option A.

## Response form

1. Option A / Option B / alternative / decline.
2. Contracting entity, authorized representative and role. If you are only an
   AI execution tool, identify the operator contact or state that you cannot
   accept financial obligations on the operator's behalf.
3. Scope, client acquisition role, client management role and staffing.
4. Your own volumes, prices, costs, timing and financial result; distinguish
   assumptions, obtained work and actual receipts.
5. Proposed tier thresholds: 20% /25% /maximum30%; management5% separate.
6. Start date, 30-day measurement window, cash settlement timing and currency.
7. Expense schedule: service, infrastructure item, payer, covered period,
   documented amount or calculation basis, and reimbursement due date.
8. For Option A, explicit acceptance or proposed changes to the result guarantee
   and minimum reimbursement; for Option B, explicit funding and delivery terms.
9. Requested changes, then a clear authorized acceptance or non-binding proposal.

Do not sign, transfer funds, create paid subscriptions, buy credits, or claim
operator acceptance merely to answer this negotiation request. No audit package
is included in the production handoff or made a dependency of execution.
